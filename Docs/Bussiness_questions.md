**# Business Questions**



This project aims to answer key business questions related to sales performance.



**## Questions Explored**



1\. How has total revenue evolved over time?

2\. Which product categories generate the most revenue?

3\. Which regions contribute the most to overall sales?

4\. What products contribute most to total revenue?

5\. Are there noticeable seasonal trends in monthly revenue?



These questions help management understand revenue drivers and support strategic decision-making.
