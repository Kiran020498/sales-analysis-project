**# Key Insights**



**## Revenue Trends**

Monthly revenue shows relatively stable performance with moderate fluctuations across months.



**## Category Performance**

The Sports category consistently generates the highest revenue, indicating strong customer demand.



**## Regional Performance**

Revenue contribution is distributed across regions, reducing dependency on a single geographic market.



**## Customer Segment**

B2B customers contribute higher revenue per account compared to other customer segments.



**## Strategic Recommendation**

The company should consider expanding the Sports product line while investigating growth opportunities in other categories to diversify revenue sources.
